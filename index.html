restio quick guide

Set the canvas dimensions or choose a preset. The preview reflects shape, color, grid or uploaded image.

  1. Workbench — adjust width, height, ratio and shape. Quick ratios keep the width fixed; Swap rotates the dimensions. Open Exact aspect ratio to enter a custom ratio.
  2. Specification library — choose a category, search formats, copy dimensions, inspect a preset or download a PNG. Social presets may include safe-zone overlays.
  3. Guideline Card Maker — open it to customize the document layout, colors and title, then copy or export the image.
  4. Paper Formats — choose DPI and display units; exports use the calculated pixel dimensions.
